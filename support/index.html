Goodnight, Stranger Support

We usually reply within two business days: labs.cluno@gmail.com

  • Messages stopped arriving: check that notifications are allowed for Goodnight, Stranger in your phone's settings. Daytime characters don't message between 23:00 and 08:00 while quiet hours are on (in the game's Settings app). The Stranger is only awake after 21:00.
  • Restore a purchase: in the game's Settings app, tap "Restore purchases" (also on the Pro screen in the Wallet app). Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. This restores Goodnight Pro; coins are kept on your phone and can't be restored after deleting the app.
  • Cancel or change your subscription: on iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Goodnight, Stranger. On Android, open Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.
  • Free trial: you won't be charged if you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play.
  • Start over: the game's Settings app → Restart story. Your coins and Pro are kept.
